Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

FamShare helps families keep track of shared subscriptions and split their cost. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and what you can do about it.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from them.
  • Your profile: your name and, only if you add them, your birth date, gender, interests, mobile number and profile photo.
  • Your family's data: the family members you add (name, emoji, colour, photo), the subscriptions you share, who is on each plan, and which payments are marked as paid.
  • Your phones: for shared logins, each phone's name and its public keys, so family members' phones can share logins with it.
  • Shared logins: stored only end-to-end encrypted. They're encrypted on your phone before they're uploaded, and only the phones you approve can decrypt them. We can't read them. We also keep a short activity log (who viewed, copied or changed a login, and when) so the people on a plan can see it.

What we use it for

Only to run FamShare: signing you in, syncing your family's list between phones, working out everyone's share, and sharing logins safely. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see it

The people in your family see the family's subscriptions, members and payments. Your birth date, gender and mobile number are not shown to your family. A shared login is only visible to the people the plan's payer shares it with.

Where it's kept

FamShare stores its data with Supabase, our hosting provider. Sign-in with Google or Apple goes through their services under their own privacy policies. Sign-in emails are sent through our email provider.

Your choices

  • You can change or remove your profile details at any time in the app, under Profile.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). This deletes your sign-in, email address and profile. A family where you're the only one with an account is deleted with its plans and payments; a family others still use stays for them.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing us.

Children

A family can add children as members without them having an account. To create an account yourself, you must be old enough to do so in your country, or have a parent's permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
